# Week 03

## TITLE:
AI-Powered Discussion Monitor and Action Item Tracker  

---

## WORK DONE:

This week, our team focused on structuring the project into a formal development plan by completing the required product planning sheets. The goal of this phase was to convert the insights gathered from customer interactions into a clear, actionable roadmap for development.

Based on the feedback collected in Week 02, we identified the core features that are essential for the system, such as topic deviation detection, toxicity monitoring, action item extraction, and meeting summarization.

We then worked on organizing these features into a structured format by creating:

- Product Overview  
- Product Backlog (with user stories and priorities)  
- Sprint Roadmap  
- Sprint Retrospective  

The backlog was carefully prepared using user stories and prioritized using the MoSCoW technique to distinguish between must-have and optional features. Following this, we created a sprint roadmap dividing the project into multiple phases, starting from MVP development to advanced features.

Additionally, we documented our learnings and reflections from the previous weeks in the retrospective section, ensuring that our planning aligns with real-world feedback and Agile methodology.

This entire planning and documentation process was primarily led by Markandeshwaragouda Patil, with support from team members in data consolidation and validation.
